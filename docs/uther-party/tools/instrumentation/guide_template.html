Uther Party Field Guide

All 90 minigames from Uther Party 4.0 and Uther Party Ultima-X, written up from the maps' own scripts, object data and terrain. The 4.0 games were also played live with 8 computer players in a logging copy of the map.

One scoring rule for every game

Each game starts with an ante of 9 − players. In a survival game every death pays the current ante and raises it by one, so with 8 players the order of death pays 1 to 7 and the survivor takes 8. Players who die in the same instant share a value. Races pay 8, 7, 6 … in finishing order; anyone who dies or runs out of time scores 0.

Engine behaviour, measured live

  • MovementFull speed instantly, no acceleration. Units stand still during an attack wind-up.
  • CollisionUnits steer around each other (40–50 u sidestep, 0.1–0.3 s lost) and never shove idle units.
  • MeleeA swing that starts at 255 u still lands at 350 u. The hit is decided when the swing begins.
  • MissilesArrows follow their target, but a runner that reversed 0.6–1.0 s into the archer's draw was hit 0 times in 60. Reversing earlier dodged almost none (20 of 24 hit).
  • ChasesA melee pursuer as fast as its prey never swings while the prey runs straight. Stop or turn, and it connects.
  • ArtilleryRocks fly at 400 u/s. Splash is exactly 1 / 0.4 / 0.25 within 25 / 50 / 150 u of the victim's edge.

Fit: 1 fits Hammerguy's Party's one-paladin-plus-Q model directly; 2 needs a new unit or a modest new mechanic; 3 needs a system the remake does not have (typing, building, economy, possession). Distances are Warcraft III units (1 tile = 128; about 54 per Hammerguy's Party unit); times are game seconds at Fast speed. Arena maps are drawn from each arena's pathing grid: dark cells are unwalkable, and outlines mark the script's regions (spawn slots, goals). Full rule sheets and the method are in docs/uther-party/ of the Hammerguy's Party repository.
